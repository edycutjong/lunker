Delete your Lunker data

Lunker (Google Play: dev.edycu.lunker) has no accounts, so a deletion request is one email. This page explains how to ask, what is deleted, and what is kept.

How to request deletion

  1. Email edy.cu@live.com with the subject “Delete my Lunker data”.
  2. If you bought anything in Lunker, include the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play receipt email, or at play.google.com → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history). This is how we find your record.
  3. If you never bought anything, tell us the approximate date you installed Lunker and your furthest lake. Your player id is random and not linked to your name or email, so we can only remove rows we can confidently match; anything we cannot match identifies no one.
  4. We delete the data listed below within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your game record on our server: player id, lakes, streak, catches, notification permission, UTC offset, and the bite send/open times for your player id.
  • Your COIN ledger rows and purchase-event rows for your player id.
  • Your OneSignal push subscription (the push token and its tags).
  • Your RevenueCat customer record (virtual-currency balance and purchase history as RevenueCat stores it).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Google Play order records (what was bought, when, and the amount) are kept by Google under Google’s own retention rules, which we do not control.
  • Where tax or accounting rules require it, a minimal purchase record (product, date, amount — no personal details) may be retained for the legally required period, then deleted.
  • Deletion does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data on your phone

Your local save lives in the app’s private storage. Uninstalling Lunker, or Android Settings → Apps → Lunker → Storage → Clear storage, deletes it immediately. Turning off Lunker notifications in Android Settings stops all bites at once.
